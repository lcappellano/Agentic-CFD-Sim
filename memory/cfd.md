# CFD notes (OpenCFD v2412; each bullet is encoded in a profile or module, listed for the why)

- `gmshToFoam` then `splitMeshRegions -cellZones -overwrite` needs root `fvSchemes`/`fvSolution` present; solid external patches must become `wall` for `wallHeatFlux`; solid needs a `p` field with heSolidThermo. (`cht_case.py`)
- Solid `h` uses PCG/DIC; multi-region parallel needs per-region `decomposeParDict`. (`cht_case.py`, `run_case.py`)
- With g = 0, `p` and `p_rgh` are absolute static pressure in Pa.
- Water Pr ≈ 6 with wall functions: use `compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction`, not plain alphatWallFunction. (`turbulence.py`)
- Aligned hex meshes: full corrected solid Laplacian, `leastSquares` gradient, 3 non-orthogonal passes (profile `hex-kepsilon`). Isotropic tets showed 22 % pressure-drop mesh sensitivity on the demo; hex O-grids removed it.
- Tetrahedral passages with sharp ribs: `leastSquares` + full correction diverged at iteration 3; `cellLimited Gauss linear 1` with 2 passes was stable (profile `tet-robust`).
- SST needs `wallDist { method meshWave; }` in fluid fvSchemes. Spalding nut + blended omega wall functions are tutorial-supported in v2412.
- A uniform initial U on a refined manifold made the first energy solve go negative; `potentialFoam -region fluid -initialiseUBCs` (U only, no phi/p) fixed the startup. (`initialize_velocity.py`, profile `tet-robust`)
- Enthalpy relaxation 0.3 in both regions made thermal transport crawl (600 iterations, 3 W pickup of 27 kW); switching to 0.9 via `numerical_variant` advanced it. 0.9 from the start was stable with potential-flow U. Profile `tet-robust-slow-energy` exists for startups that diverge at 0.9.
- Fields are only saved at write times: chunk ends must land on a write; `run_case` adjusts `writeInterval` with a gcd.
- The polynomial transport (`icoPolynomial`/`hPolynomial`) has no range clamp in the solver; the audits reject out-of-range temperatures. Fit 278.15–440 K covered inlet undershoots that a 283.15 lower bound did not.
- Never source the OpenFOAM bashrc after `set -e` in a shell; it exits. The pipeline sources it in Python instead.
- Laminar regions need only U, p, p_rgh, T (installed v2412 externalCoupledHeater tutorial); `simulationType laminar` with no k/omega/nut/alphat. The gates drop the turbulence residuals for that model (`turbulence.residual_equations`). The driver picks the `laminar` profile from the prescreen regime unless the spec names one.
- Laminar CHT on the 1 mm square-channel bar (Re 47, 9 W): enthalpy relaxation 0.9 gave an energy-balance e-folding of ~3000 iterations; relaxation 1.0 in both regions closed the balance within 500 iterations from the same checkpoint (`numerical_variant`, run 20260918T215315). Profile `laminar` now uses 1.0.
- Solid `h` with `relTol 0.001` left a linear *final* residual of ~2e-6, failing the 1e-7 linear-final gate; `cht_case` solves it with `relTol 0`, absolute 1e-10. That did not move the *initial* residual floor (2.4e-3): the cause was the `cellLimited Gauss linear 1` solid gradient feeding the explicit non-orthogonal correction, which settled into a period-3 limit cycle (solid min T flickering by 1.5 mK) on the tet mesh. With `Gauss linear` the same checkpoint reached 7e-11 in 150 iterations and the field moved < 1 mK; `leastSquares` also converged but shifted the peak by 0.1 K. Profile `laminar` uses `Gauss linear`; `tet-robust` keeps the limiter for startup stability, so switch the solid gradient once its field is stable if the residual gate blocks.
- `initialization.temperature_from_prescreen: true` seeds the solid at the prescreen spread estimate and the fluid at the mean of inlet and estimated outlet; it shortens the pseudo-transient and never changes the converged result.
- Manifold copper 40 L/min (run 20260918T230613, 2.86 M tets, 0.2 mm channel box): the fluid residual floor (p_rgh 1e-2, U 6e-4, k 1e-3, gate 1e-5) is flat from iteration 250 to 3000 and identical at 24/30/40 L/min while the heated maximum settles to 0.05 K and the energy balance closes to 0.04 %: a SIMPLE limit cycle of the isotropic tet mesh, not an under-iterated case. More iterations do nothing; `report.py` now says so when only the residual/pressure-drift gates fail, and suggests the restart or a finer mesh instead.
- Restart under changed numerics: `initialization.fields_from_case` (`warm_start.seed_fields`) copies every field the two cases share (U, p, p_rgh, T, k, omega, nut, alphat; never phi) from a settled case on the same mesh and skips potentialFoam; the operating point must match. Profile `tet-robust-restart` (unlimited solid gradient) exists for it; the fixture e2e test covers the path. Same run directory with `--run` keeps the mesh cached.
- Hand check for channel parts before trusting a CFD pressure drop: 40 L/min in 17 channels of 1 x 2 mm (D_h 1.33 mm, 19.9 m/s, Re 26 000) gives friction of about one dynamic head over 52 mm plus about 1.5 heads for a sharp entrance and exit, 1.92 bar per head, so about 4.8 bar per channel; the 2.83 bar of the 0.2 mm tet mesh (5 cells across the gap) is 1.5 heads for the whole part. The prescreen's 2.64 bar uses the passage-averaged D_h of 3.86 mm and is not a confirmation.
- Astra manifold geometry: the channels end at |x| = 26 mm and the headers behind them are a 1.6 mm high slot (y 0.7 to 2.3 mm at |x| = 30 mm) that the bulk size meshes with 3 to 5 cells; a channel refinement box must cover |x| up to 34 mm and y 0.6 to 2.4 mm (spec `manifold-copper-40lpm-fine`, 0.12 mm).
- `cucrzr` (k 320 W/m K, ITER-grade values) is in `materials.SOLIDS` so the SU2 report (CuCrZr) can be compared like for like; copper runs about 9 K lower on the heated face.
- Gmsh serial Delaunay (Algorithm3D 1) at 17.9 M cells (0.12 mm box, 0.6 mm transition to a 1.5 mm bulk, run 20260921T194324) left 32 sliver tets (aspect ratio 1e5, SICN ~1e-5) in the copper ribs and one face with skewness 4.3; checkMesh rejected the solid after a 14 min mesh and a 9 min case build. `mesh_regions` now gates every mesh on a minimum SICN (default 0.01) with a Netgen pass as fallback and fails the mesh stage instead; `algorithm_3d 10` (HXT, threaded) with a wider transition is the setting for large boxes.
- Fine tet mesh startup (7.6 M cells, 0.15 mm channel box, run 20260921T201334 case-0ba8adbb): from the potential-flow start, tet-robust (U 0.3, p 0.2, h 0.9) overshot to 166 m/s and -2000 bar at iteration 10, held continuity errors of 150-400 through iteration 450 instead of decaying (the 2.86 M mesh had 102 at 100 and 43 at 300) and then failed with a negative fluid temperature in the enthalpy solve. A slow-start profile (h 0.3, U 0.2, p 0.1) was tried next and diverged faster (see below); the answer is the mapped start. At iteration 100 that mesh already showed 4.87 bar (unconverged) against 2.83 bar converged on the coarse mesh.
- Lower relaxation is the wrong lever for a potential-flow start on a fine mesh: U 0.2 / p 0.1 / h 0.3 (case-3dbd6604) let the corner spikes grow (191 to 1347 m/s, T to 106 K by iteration 70) where tet-robust's 0.3 / 0.2 had damped them; the profile was removed. The right start for a refined mesh is the converged coarse solution mapped onto it: `initialization.fields_mapped_from_case` (`src/cfd/map_fields.py`, mapFields -consistent per region, mapNearest).
- Mapped start works: the 0.2 mm solution mapped onto the 7.6 M cell mesh (run 20260921T201334 case-6c35ea4c, mapNearest, 4 min) ran tet-robust stably from iteration 1 (energy closed to 0.03 % by 600 iterations, heated maximum within 3 K of the coarse value from the first chunk); the potential-flow start on the same mesh had diverged twice.
- Sharp channel-entrance edges are mesh-dependent cavitation flags: on the 0.15 mm mesh one cell at the inlet-side entrance edge of the outermost channel (x 26.05 mm, y 1.44 mm, z -23.03 mm) sits at -2.0 bar absolute with a 48 m/s local peak (dynamic head 11 bar against a 10.7 bar inlet static); the 0.2 mm mesh had +1.85 bar there and 44 m/s. The liquid screen stops the case after two consecutive chunks by design; that is an operating-point or screen-policy decision for the user, not a numerics fix.
- Mesh pair at 40 L/min, 8 bar, copper: 0.2 mm (3000 it) 2.83 bar / 154.4 C vs 0.15 mm (600 it, still creeping up from 2.61) 2.67 bar / 151.4 C. Isotropic refinement at wall-function resolution (y+ mean 52 to 41) moves the pressure drop down, not toward the report's 4.97 bar; the remaining suspects are wall-function friction on tets (prism layers or a straight-channel friction benchmark against Petukhov decide it) and the report's single-channel model definition. The `mesh_*` acceptance keys are not evaluated by code; the pair is compared by hand.
- Astra manifold rib ends are concave V-notches (apex at |x| = 26 mm at mid-height, opening to the full 1 mm channel height at |x| = 26.5, two knife-edged prongs per rib), not wedges; the -2 bar cell of the fine mesh sits at that apex. The headers are tapered plenums (8 x 8 mm at |x| = 59 to 1 x 50 mm at |x| = 28, a 68 degree fan in plan) with no step into the channel band; the 0.88 outer-channel share comes from the fan, not from a contraction. Variants built 2026-09-22 with `src/cad/variants.py`: `inputs/astra-manifold-variants/Astra_Manifold_bullnose.STEP` (32 rib ends filled and rounded, +4.6 mm3) and `..._bullnose-vanes2.STEP` (plus 2 vanes per side in both headers, 0.4 mm, x 57 to 31 mm, +405 mm3); both import as one solid. gmsh needs a lowercase .step extension to export, its boolean return lists are unreliable (read the model's volumes after each boolean), and `intersect` with a thin slab returns the whole sub-solid: measure passage widths with `isInside` marching instead.
- Smooth-tube benchmark (run 20260922T163313, `specs/benchmark-smooth-tube-re26k.json`, generated part `inputs/benchmarks/smooth-tube-d2-l100`, 2 mm bore, L/D 50, Re 26 000, 0.3 mm isotropic tets in the bore, y+ about 50, tet-robust, 102 k cells, 2000 iterations in 85 s): developed Darcy friction 0.0263 vs Petukhov 0.0245 (+7.6 %, Blasius +5.6 %) and developed Nusselt 176 vs Gnielinski 187 (-5.8 %). The wall-function tetrahedral treatment is fine on a straight round duct at the manifold's resolution. Reduction: `src.verification.duct_benchmark`. The 1 % port-area audit fails on a 2 mm bore meshed with 24 curvature points (polygon area -1.1 %); harmless here.
- Manifold channel friction is genuinely low and it is not the wall functions: the static-pressure gradient fitted inside the channels (|x| < 10 mm, entrance recovery excluded) gives f 0.0164 on the 0.2 mm mesh, 0.0160 on the 0.15 mm mesh, 0.0191 on Revision B, against Petukhov 0.0245 (-33 / -35 / -22 %), uniform along the channel and worst in the outer channels. Turbulence in the channels matches the tube (nut/nu 17.6 vs 18.8, Tu 6.6 vs 6.8 %), so it is not laminarization. Remaining suspects: the 1 x 2 mm section with 0.1 mm corner fillets (curvature-refined 0.03 mm corner cells next to 0.2 mm wall cells) and the separated, turned feed from the header. The discriminating test is a straight 1 x 2 mm filleted channel at 19.6 m/s with the manifold mesh settings (generator to add beside `smooth_tube_handoff`).
