Source and geometry notes
    1 · DEFINE

    Simulation requirements

    Review only. This step does not create a simulation mesh or solve the part. CAD cannot determine your heat load or plumbing direction.

    Materials and temperatures

    Temperature limit applies to the orange selected heated surfaces. Enter absolute temperatures in kelvin.

    Heating

    Uniform heat flux into the solid on every orange surface.

    Coolant operating bounds

    Coolant enters the blue inlet set and exits the green outlet set. Use equal lower and upper values for a fixed operating point.

    Both absolute-pressure bounds must be greater than zero, with lower ≤ upper. For a fixed pressure, enter the same value in both boxes.

    Goal and scope

    Initial scope: steady operation, one solid material, one coolant, uniform heating and fixed geometry.
